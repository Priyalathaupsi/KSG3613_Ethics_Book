# Ethics in Practice

Open course book for KSG3613 Introduction to Ethics, laid out as a book: cover, foreword, contents, four chapters and an appendix.

Instructor: Dr. Priyalatha Govindasamy, Lecturer, Department of Psychology, Faculty of Human Development, Universiti Pendidikan Sultan Idris (gpriyalatha@fpm.upsi.edu.my).

Source: Welfel, E. R. (2016). *Ethics in counseling and psychotherapy: Standards, research, and emerging issues* (6th ed.). Cengage Learning.

## Changing the guide picture
Save your own picture as `guide.png` (a square image works best) and upload it over the existing one. It appears on the cover and every page.

## Chapter 4 escape room results
When students finish `chapter4_escape_room.html`, they click Submit and their score is emailed to gpriyalatha@fpm.upsi.edu.my through FormSubmit. The very first submission triggers a one time activation email from FormSubmit; click the link in it. If you later set up the Google Sheet route (Code.gs), paste the web app URL into `SCRIPT_URL` near the top of the script and results go to the sheet instead.
